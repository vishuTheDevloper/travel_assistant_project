# Things To Do & Must-Visit Places in Singapore

Source: https://www.visitsingapore.com/things-to-do/top-things-to-do/
Accessed: 2026-09-14

## Top Things To Do

The best things to do in Singapore, curated for first timers and repeat visitors alike. Discover iconic attractions, must-visit places and unique experiences across the city, from famous sights to memorable activities for every kind of traveller.

## A Noodle Story

Taste the fusion of flavours in handmade noodles, blending local and international influences.

## Adventure Cove

Have a splashing good time at Adventure Cove, experience thrilling water slides, snorkel with fishes and relax on the lazy river.

## AltitudeX

Defy gravity, soar indoors and feel the adrenaline of flight at AltitudeX on Sentosa Island.

## Andsoforth

Experience immersive dining adventures with ever-changing themes and theatrical elements.

## ArtScience Museum

Discover where art and science intersect with a wide range of exhibitions and events within the lotus-inspired building.

## Asian Civilisations Museum

Journey through Asia's diverse cultures, exploring art, artifacts and captivating exhibitions.

## Big Bus Singapore Night City Tour

Zip through Singapore's streets at night on an open-top double decker bus on Big Bus' Night City Tour.

## Bird Paradise

Take flight to Asia's largest bird park, home to more than 3,500 birds from over 400 species. Explore 10 immersive walk-through aviaries, inspired by habitats from around the world.

## Bounce Singapore

Bounce, flip and play on trampolines, bringing active fun to the whole family.

## Bynd Artisan

Elevate your lifestyle with luxury handcrafted leather goods and thoughtful personalised gifts.

## CHIJMES

Enter into an enchanting world, where history meets elegance. Marvel at the stunning architecture of this restored convent turned dining and entertainment complex.

## Cable Car Dining

Dine amidst breathtaking views aboard a cable car, soaring over Sentosa Island and the cityscape.

## Caffe Fernet

A modern Italian restaurant and bar on the waterfront, serving moreish plates and handmade pastas, with stunning views of Marina Bay.

## Changi Chapel and Museum

Commemorate wartime history and take in poignant stories of bravery and resilience at the Changi Chapel and Museum.

## Children's Museum

Ignite your child’s curiosity at the Children's museum which offers immersive exhibits and interactive experiences for young minds.

## Chinatown

Embrace Chinese heritage through bustling streets, vibrant culture and delectable cuisine in Chinatown.

## Curiosity Cove

Discover Singapore’s largest indoor nature-inspired playscape for kids 12 and under. Through interactive and educational play, children explore how animals live, adapt and thrive in the wild across four immersive zones filled with wonder and discovery.

## Design Orchard

Discover local designers' creations and stylish souvenirs at Design Orchard.

## Disney Cruise Line

Adventure is calling! With 7 uniquely themed areas brimming with fun and relaxation, world-class entertainment and signature Disney dining, the all-new Disney Adventure will set sail from Singapore. Bookings now open.

## Draft Land

Sip on a locally-inspired drink at this first-ever bar in Singapore, serving cocktails on tap.

## Eastern Coastal Loop Night Cycling

Adventure on wheels along the Eastern Coastal Loop under the stars with your family and friends.

## Esplanade

Discover captivating performances and embrace the unique, durian-inspired architecture that sets the Esplanade apart.

## Exploria

Explore one of Southeast Asia's largest indoor, nature-themed multimedia attractions, where rarely seen realms of the world come to life. Use your RFID wristband to unlock interactive touchpoints and discover the wonders of nature — past and present.

## Forest Bathing by Forest with You

Embrace nature's healing touch, immerse in lush greenery and rejuvenate amidst the serenity of a forest.

## Fort Canning Park

Uncover ancient relics and historical gardens amidst the urban bustle or catch the occasional concert or theatre production happening.

## Funan

A tech paradise where innovation meets lifestyle, Funan offers cutting-edge gadgets and hip fashion finds.

## GINLEE

Discover contemporary fashion pieces and accessories, merging minimalist aesthetics with Singaporean flair.

## Gardens by the Bay

Marvel at the fusion of urban architecture and lush greenery in this breathtaking garden.

## Gx-5 Extreme Swing

Brave the Gx-5 Extreme Swing for an adrenaline-pumping thrill amidst the city's nighttime glow.

## Hajjah Fatimah Mosque

Experience a mix of Islamic and European architectural styles, rich in heritage and spirituality.

## Hell’s Museum

Explore perspectives on life’s biggest mysteries across religions, cultures, and civilizations at this museum dedicated to death and the afterlife. Located in Haw Par Villa, it offers an immersive experience that includes the infamous 10 Courts of Hell.

## Henderson Waves Bridge

Experience the stunning wave-like design at the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore. Be awed by panoramic views and observe a rich variety of flora and fauna.

## Home of Seafood

A seafood paradise with tantalising dishes and fresh catches, Home of Seafood will delight all seafood lovers.

## ION Orchard

Shop luxury brands and international boutiques amidst stunning architecture at ION Orchard.

## Intertidal Trails at Changi

Uncover marine wonders and coastal serenity through the intertidal trails at Changi.

## Jewel Changi Airport

Experience nature's splendor with a stunning indoor waterfall amidst world-class shopping and dining at Jewel Changi.

## Kampong Gelam

Explore rich history, living traditions, excellent eateries and trendy shops in this vibrant neighborhood.

## Katong-Joo Chiat

Discover the enchanting blend of Peranakan heritage, charming streets, and boutiques in the Katong-Joo Chiat neighborhood.

## Kranji Marshes

Escape to the serene beauty of Kranji Marshes, immerse yourself in lush wetlands, diverse wildlife, and tranquil trails.

## Kranji War Memorial

Pay respects to the fallen soldiers at Kranji War Memorial, and find solace in this solemn and poignant site.

## Kulnari Mystery Golf

Solve intriguing puzzles, play mini golf and unravel thrilling mysteries in this immersive mystery golf course.

## Labyrinth

Take your tastebuds on an adventure at this Michelin-starred restaurant, which uses local flavours and homegrown produce.

## Lau Pa Sat

This historic hawker centre will take you on a gastronomic adventure of diverse flavours and vibrant atmosphere.

## Lazarus Island

Bask in the sun, sand, and sea at Lazarus Island. This hidden gem boasts relaxing beaches and abundant marine life, just a ferry ride away from the mainland.

## Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum

Marvel at nature's wonders, encounter dinosaurs, fossils and other diverse specimens. Immerse yourself in a world of biodiversity and conservation.

## Little India

Step into the vibrant tapestry of Singapore's Little India, an Indian cultural haven brimming with heritage, excitement, and endless charm.

## MADLY Gems

Embark on a journey of precious gemstones and bespoke jewelry craftsmanship.

## MINT Museum of Toys

Home to Asia’s largest collection of vintage toys and collectibles, this museum showcases childhood treasures from over 50 countries. Visitors can look forward to an interactive AI museum ambassador, and rediscover the wonder of play.

## Mandai Gallery

Step into Mandai Gallery, an exhibition that celebrates the history and transformation of the Mandai Wildlife Reserve. Learn about how the reserve has contributed to nation-building, its commitment to sustainability and its efforts in wildlife conservation both locally and regionally.

## Mandai Wildlife Reserve

Observe the beauty of nature in action at Mandai Wildlife Reserve and learn how we are creating a better future for our wildlife and planet.

## Marina Bay Sands

Experience luxury and breathtaking views at Marina Bay Sands. Indulge in world-class entertainment, dining, and the iconic rooftop infinity pool.

## Mr Bucket Chocolaterie Dempsey Factory

Indulge in chocolate heaven, with engaging workshops, an open-concept production space, and exquisite bean-to-bar chocolate.

## Mustafa Centre

Shop around the clock for almost anything, from electronics, to textiles and jewelry.

## Mutiara Seafood

Indulge in the freshest seafood at Mutiara Seafood, where authentic Singaporean cuisine and Halal-certified dishes celebrate tradition.

## NOX Dine in the Dark

Experience 12 mystery dishes served by visually impaired waiters in complete darkness. Be ready for a multi-sensory dining experience that will tantalise your taste buds and open your mind.

## National Gallery Singapore

Discover the world's largest public display of modern Southeast Asian art within a restored City Hall and former Supreme Court building.

## National Museum of Singapore

Uncover the nation's history, from colonial times to modern Singapore through immersive exhibits.

## Naturalist Night Adventure

Embark on a thrilling wildlife safari adventure, spotting nocturnal creatures under the moonlit sky at Sentosa Island.

## New Bahru

Built on the former Nan Chiau High School, New Bahru at River Valley celebrates heritage through retail, dining, and lifestyle experiences by over 40 homegrown brands.

## Night Safari

Discover the world's first nocturnal wildlife park, a one-of-a-kind experience that unveils the mysteries of the animal kingdom at night. Embark on a journey by tram or on foot and get up-close to over 900 nocturnal animals.

## No.5 Emerald Hill Cocktail Bar

Sip delightful cocktails at No.5 Emerald Hill Cocktail Bar, a stylish bar with a nostalgic charm.

## Old Hill Street Police Station

Admire the vibrant rainbow hues which add a touch of charm to this historic building, now home to various government agencies.

## Orchard Road

Explore Orchard Road's shopping haven, the ultimate destination for retail therapy and luxury fashion.

## Peranakan Musuem

Delve into the vibrant Peranakan culture and explore the traditional art and artifacts at the Peranakan Museum.

## Pulau Ubin

Step back in time on this island with cycling trails, kayaking and a rustic charm like no other.

## Raffles Hotel

Step back in time to experience colonial architecture where luxury and heritage seamlessly blend together at the Raffles Hotel.

## Rainforest Wild Adventure

Glide through the canopy or swing high into the sky with Adventure+ activities, taking in heart-racing views while spotting animals beneath. Don't miss the exclusive Okapi and Diana Monkey, now in Singapore for the first time, and many more!

## Rempapa

Enjoy Singapore’s diverse cuisine with Chef Damian, whose culinary creations resonate with diners across generations.

## River Wonders

Immerse yourself in Asia's first and only river-themed wildlife park, home to one of the world's largest collections of freshwater vertebrates. Explore iconic rivers and encounter resident giants like the pandas, Mekong catfish and more.

## Royal Albatross

Dine and sail on an evening cruise with Royal Albatross, or lunch at the Albatross Island Restaurant in the Southern Islands, reachable by Albatross speedboats from Marina South Pier, Resorts World Sentosa, or Ola Beach Club.

## Science Centre

Explore a world of discovery at Science Centre Singapore, where hands-on exhibits, imaginative play at KidsSTOP™, and immersive 8K 3D dome experiences at the Omni-Theatre inspire curious minds of all ages to discover, learn, and imagine together.

## Sentosa Island

Beach play, thrilling attractions and family time await you at Sentosa Island.

## Singapore Art Museum

Encounter contemporary Southeast Asian art, expressing cultural nuances and artistic brilliance.

## Singapore Botanic Gardens

A harmonious blend of flora and fauna, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is vibrant with history.

## Singapore Discovery Centre

Unleash your curiosity, explore interactive exhibits and discover the wonders of a multi-sensory learning experience as a family.

## Singapore Flyer

Soar above the city on Asia's largest observation wheel and marvel at panoramic views of Marina Bay.

## Singapore Maritime Gallery

Uncover Singapore’s rich maritime heritage and how it is a leading maritime centre, through simulations, tours, exhibitions and more.

## Singapore Oceanarium

Dive into a mesmerizing underwater world with over 40,000 marine animals across 120 species. Encounter deep-sea creatures, sea jellies and witness the magic of the ocean.

## Singapore River Cruise

Cruise the Singapore River on an iconic bumboat ride at night, taking in stunning cityscapes.

## Singapore Sidecar Tour

Uncover Singapore's heritage in a vintage Vespa sidecar and feel the wind in your face while exploring cultural landmarks.

## Singapore Zoo

Visit Singapore's only rainforest zoo, renowned for its naturalist, cageless habitats. From the furry and adorable to the ferocious and majestic, the animals of the Singapore Zoo are a sight to behold.

## Spectra - A Light & Water Show

Be mesmerized by the captivating light and water show taking place daily at Marina Bay Sands.

## St John's Island

Bask in the sun, sand, and sea at St. John's Island. This hidden gem boasts relaxing beaches and abundant marine life, just a ferry ride away from the mainland.

## Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve

Discover wildlife, lush mangroves and serene landscapes at this captivating wetland sanctuary.

## Synthesis Bar

Savour mixology magic, tasting eclectic concoctions amidst chic and vibrant surroundings.

## TANGS Orchard

A beloved department store in Singapore, TANGS offers a curated selection of fashion, beauty and homeware.

## The Istana

Get a glimpse of the official residence of the President of the Republic of Singapore through guided tours of its majestic architecture and history.

## The Southern Ridges

Embark on a scenic hike through lush treetop walks and iconic city vistas. Catch the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore and its iconic wave-like structure.

## Trifecta

Experience the ultimate thrill and unleash your adventurous spirit at Trifecta. Hit the snowboard, ride the waves, and feel the excitement of the skate bowl.

## Ubin Bisect Kayaking

Glide through serene mangroves and encounter diverse wildlife, connecting with nature at Pulau Ubin.

## Universal Studios Singapore

Experience the magic and thrills at Universal Studios Singapore, where blockbuster movies come to life in a world of endless entertainment.

## VivoCity

Indulge in a retail, dining and lifestyle haven with waterfront relaxation and an endless mix of entertainment.

## Wild Wild Wet

Slide, splash and have an exciting water park adventure with endless excitement for the whole family.

## Zouk

Get your groove on at Zouk, Singapore's iconic nightclub, known for epic dance parties.

## Are You A Solo Traveller?

Find the best experiences in Singapore, from daytime escapes to nights out alone.
