# Question

Which local dishes should I try at Singapore hawker centres?

### Information from the travel sources

- Chinese options available at hawker centres include bak chor mee (minced pork noodles tossed in chilli, lard, and toppings), Hainanese chicken rice, fried Hokkien mee, bak kut teh, and prawn mee. [P1]
- Laksa is a well-known dish featuring rice noodles in a rich coconut-curry broth with cockles or shrimp, commonly served spicy in hawker centres. [P2]
- Mee siam consists of rice flour noodles in a sweet-sour tamarind and fermented bean broth with bean curd and boiled eggs, commonly available at Malay stalls. [P2]
- Hawker centres also feature a wide selection of Indian dishes. [P3]

### Suggested plan or choices

- Consider ordering your noodle dishes 'dry' to have them tossed in chilli and seasonings with broth provided separately. [P1]
- If you are sensitive to heat, you can ask stallholders for less or no chilli when ordering laksa. [P2]
