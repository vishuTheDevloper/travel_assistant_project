# Explore Singapore in 4 Days: Ultimate Itinerary & Tours!

## 4 Days in Singapore

In town for a while? Shop, eat, and play with this detailed itinerary and enjoy the best of Singapore.

## Day 1: Explore the City

### Morning

#### Kampong Gelam

Start your day with breakfast at this conserved area, where Malay royalty once resided, and is steeped in rich culture and history.

#### Zam Zam Restaurant

Wake your taste buds up with a classic Singapore-style breakfast, by dropping by Singapore Zam Zam Restaurant for prata (South Indian flatbread) and a side of curry.

#### Hygge

Head to Haji Lane —a hipster haunt littered with indie outlets that stock vintage clothing and knick-knacks—such as home and lifestyle store Hygge.

### Afternoon

#### Civic District

Come midday, make your way to the Civic District. Pop by Raffles Hotel for lunch, known for its rich history and exquisite colonial architecture.

#### Long Bar

Explore the timeless allure of Long Bar, renowned for being the birthplace of the legendary Singapore Sling cocktail.

#### National Gallery Singapore

Then, pay a visit to National Gallery Singapore to marvel at the world's largest public display of modern Southeast Asian art.

### Evening

#### Singapore River

Just before dinner, hop on a traditional bumboat by Singapore River Cruise and get to know the historic Singapore River.

#### Offtrack

Art, food and music come together at Offtrack, an effortlessly cool bar that serves up Pan Asian cuisine, cocktails and live DJ sets.

#### PLUME

A cocktail bar inspired by the birds of Singapore, PLUME serves up intriguing cocktails and appetisers.

## Day 2: Visit Neighborhoods

### Morning

#### Katong-Joo Chiat

Nestled in the East, with its rainbow-hued heritage shophouses, hip cafes and modern boutiques, this is one of Singapore’s most charming districts.

#### Chin Mee Chin Confectionery

Indulge in an old-school Singapore style breakfast with the fluffiest buns and thick spreads of homemade coconut kaya jam.

#### Kim Choo Kueh Chang

From stunning traditional wares and unique souvenirs, this beloved store is your one-stop shop for discovering the rich tapestry of Peranakan culture.

### Afternoon

#### Chinatown

Once a Chinese immigrant enclave, today's Chinatown is a mix of old and new, with historic temples alongside award-winning bars and restaurants.

#### Maxwell Food Centre

Visit Maxwell Food Centre for local chicken rice from Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice, and grab a cold glass of sugarcane juice to quench your thirst.

#### Buddha Tooth Relic Temple

Continue by foot and discover Chinatown's rich culture and history at the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple.

### Evening

#### Marina Bay

An iconic district, immerse in cityscape views, stunning architecture and trendy dining spots.

#### The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands

With over 170 luxury brands – from Tiffany & Co to Acne Studio's first-in-Asia store – The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands is a great place to indulge in a bout of retail therapy.

#### Red House Seafood

Dig into a plate of chill crab – Singapore's most iconic seafood dish – and indulge in a variety of classic sharing dishes.

## Day 3: Be One with Nature (plus a spot of shopping)

### Morning

#### Singapore Botanic Gardens

Enjoy lush gardens, peace and tranquility at this 150-year-old green destination–which happens to be Singapore’s first UNESCO World Heritage Site.

#### National Orchid Garden

Don’t miss the National Orchid Garden, home to the largest orchid display in the world with over 60,000 plants and orchids on display.

#### The Halia

Break for brunch - located amidst the one-hectare Ginger Garden, this restaurant specialises in contemporary European cuisine.

### Afternoon

#### Orchard Road

Switch up the pace – head to Singapore’s major shopping district, where you’ll find a mix of high-end brands, local labels, and everything in between.

#### Design Orchard

Experience a curation of over 100 homegrown fashion and lifestyle labels, the largest one-stop destination to shop all things local.

#### Violet Oon

Tantalise your tastebuds with Peranakan cuisine. This homegrown dining is famous for serving up authentic dishes, from dry laksa to aromatic curries.

### Evening

#### Bugis Street Market

Explore Bugis, a district with a colorful past and is now known for its trendy stores. Find great bargains and more at Bugis Street Market.

#### Little India and Banana Leaf Apolo

Make a beeline for the ethnic enclave Little India, and tuck into mouth-watering South Indian food such as popular local delicacy fish head curry at Banana Leaf Apolo.

#### Mustafa Centre

Itching for more shopping options? Check out Mustafa Centre–a shopping complex that sells just about everything you can think of. Best of all, it’s open till late night.

## Day 4: Animals Galore

### Morning

#### Mandai

Make your way to the Northern area of Singapore to be enthralled by the abundance of flora and fauna in our world-class attractions.

#### Bird Paradise

Home to over 3,500 birds, be immersed in naturalistic walk-through aviaries that reflect different habitats of the world.

#### Singapore Zoo

Alternatively, head to an award-winning wildlife park home to more than 2,400 animals across 300 species, and encounter free-roaming animals.

### Evening

#### Mandai Wildlife West

Play, dine or enjoy a quick respite from your wildlife adventure, from the towering waterfall upon arrival to the interactive play areas.

#### Evening in the Wild

Dig into a four-course dinner in Singapore's largest Tipi Tent and embark on a tram ride to discover the mysterious creatures of the night.

#### Night Safari

For your final stop, hop on over to the Night Safari. This park welcomes guests to the world’s first nocturnal safari experience with over 900 animals.
